What I found

The fixes that matter most

I opened all 19 pages of cartzillaride.com two ways: the way a visitor sees them, and the raw file Google and AI tools get first.

<title>Cartzilla Ride | Las Vegas Strip Tour Guide Experience</title> ...tracking codes... <body> <div id="root"></div> <- the whole page, on every address </body>
01

Every page starts as the same empty box

All 19 pages, and even made-up addresses, send the identical file: the homepage title, no headline, zero words and no business info. Your real content only appears after code runs. Google runs it late and less reliably. ChatGPT and most AI search tools never run it, so to them your whole site is one blank page.

Webmaster
02

Broken links show the homepage

A fake address like /zzz-not-a-page loads your homepage with a "200 OK" instead of a "not found" page. Google counts these as errors, and junk addresses pile up as copies of your homepage.

Webmaster
03

The http:// version is down

http://cartzillaride.com returns a 500 server error. Anyone who types it without https, or follows an old link, lands on an error page.

Webmaster
04

Share previews are broken

When someone shares your site, the preview image points at an old test site, cartzilla-redesign--calebgay7.replit.app. Once the page loads, it points at a logo address that is not actually an image. Every page also tells Facebook it is the homepage.

Webmaster
05

Your pickup ZIP disagrees with itself

Every page shows "Pick-up On Strip, Las Vegas, NV 89107". The hidden business info Google reads says 89109, and the map pin sits by Treasure Island. Two addresses make Google trust you less.

Search Visibility
06

Your site undersells your reviews

The hidden business info claims 5.0 stars from 50 reviews. Out in the world you have 4.9 stars from 558 on Viator, 221 on Tripadvisor and 892+ on Groupon. Your best selling point is missing.

Search Visibility
07

Four pages chase one search

"Group activities", "fun group activities", "unique group activities" and "things to do with a group" all aim at nearly the same Google search, so they compete with each other instead of lifting each other.

Search Visibility
08

Heavy pages, missing descriptions

The homepage downloads 34.6 MB and each party page about 20 MB. On the Strip Tour page, 124 of 141 images have no description for Google.

Search Visibility
09

Five pages Google is not told about

Your sitemap leaves out the fun, unique and things-to-do group pages, plus /construction and /franchise.

Search Visibility
10

Pages people search for that don't exist

There is no page for reviews, private tours, pricing, pickup directions or a general FAQ. These are the questions people ask Google and ChatGPT before they book.

What I'd fix first

In this order

  1. Send every page fully written

    Change how the site is delivered so Google and AI tools get each page's real headline, text and business info right away, not an empty box.

  2. Fix the plumbing

    Real "not found" pages, a working http:// redirect, and share previews that show your cart on every page.

  3. One pickup address everywhere

    Match the visible ZIP, the hidden business info and the map pin.

  4. Put your real reviews to work

    Show your 4.9 stars and review counts on the site and tell Google about them.

  5. Sharpen and fill the gaps

    Give each group page its own search, lighten the heavy pages, describe the images, complete the sitemap, and add reviews, private tours, pricing, pickup and FAQ pages.
